fix(version): sync GUI_VERSION dynamically with package.json and electron app.getVersion

Previously GUI_VERSION was hardcoded to 1.9.157 in src/version.ts and electron/version.ts.
This caused the Electron client to report 1.9.157 while the C++ engine was 1.9.161,
triggering repeated update prompts. Bump all targets to v1.9.162 with dynamic version resolution.
